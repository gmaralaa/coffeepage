Our story

A Little Shop With a Big Love for Coffee

How it started

Coffee Shop began with a simple idea: everyone has a taste of their own, and a good cup should help you find it. On a rainy Portland morning in 2026 we opened the doors of a tiny corner space on Maple Street with one espresso machine, a handful of tables and a lot of nerves.

Within a week the tables were full. Neighbors turned into regulars, regulars turned into friends, and the corner quickly became the place people stopped before work, after school and on slow Sunday afternoons.

From the farm to your cup

We buy our beans in small batches from farms we trust and roast them every week, so what you drink is always fresh. We talk to the people who grow the coffee, pay them fairly and choose beans for flavor first, not for the label.

Then it is our turn. We grind to order, pull every shot by hand and pour the milk slowly, because the last few seconds of a coffee are the ones you taste.

Find your taste. Not ours, not a trend. Yours.

We are still a small shop, and we like it that way. We still know most of our guests by name, we still change the menu with the seasons and we still believe that a good coffee is the easiest way to slow down for a minute.

Come in, pick a table by the window and tell us how you like your coffee. We would love to be part of your story, too.
